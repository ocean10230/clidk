### Auto click:
+ A regular auto click BUT there's chance of malfunctioning (for a short duration from 4-20s based on the upgrade level)
+ Upgrade "Random Events" to reduce the malfunctioning duration. Max 2s-5s
+ Adjustable interval from 0.2-5s, making auto click running with extreme low level may breaks them very often unless the upgrade's level is high enough
+ Formula: $ = (CM / 2 - level) / interval

### Botting:
+ A network of bots registered under your name, Level = Amount of bot, rewards from all bots will be claimed every 8m automatically
+ The network of bots can be easily detected if you have too many bots doing the same task, chances cannot be reduced - be careful
+ Once a bot is found, it will be traced back (5s-20s), you can upgrade black-hat hackers to increase tracing from 10-50s and reducing successful tracing chance
+ You are given money and gems (hopefully) but all rewards are cut into halves

### Black-hat hackers:
+ A group of black-hat hackers are recruited, very expensive. They actively tries finding vulnerabilities, whether small or critical, all can bring you goods
+ Every 1m there is a chance of finding vulnerables that are used for exploit:

- Vulnerabilities are ranged from *Low* to *Critical*
> Low: 50-80%; Medium: 30-50%; High: 15-30%; Critical:

- After the vulnerable is found, they will attempt to exploit the system, duration ranged from: 20s-3m based on severity, to reduce the time, upgrade them.
- After they've exploited, depending on the level of the upgrade and the serverity, you'll get money, gems, a bit of bitcoin.

- During and after the breaching, the team will attempt tracing back, interval: 2.5m/search and chances are constant: 20%
- Even if they don't find you, they may randomly attempt tracing back with the same principle above every 1m with a chance of 1/1000
- If you get caught, your hackers face punishments and the hacking team will be disabled for around 5-10m (based on the breaches you've made). If you're punishment time is low (>7 mins), you can bribe the administrator (hopefully), if you success then your suspension is lifted, if not then good luck with an extra 2 minutes
